## ILLUSTRATION

No freely licensed photo was found on Wikimedia Commons for these products (searched 25 Nov 2026), so each is an original drawing of the generic form factor only. They are hand-written SVG (solid indigo #201e37 shapes with a programmatic 45-degree halftone dot screen), generated by a Python script and rasterised with rsvg-convert. No AI image model, no logos, no text, no tracing of product imagery. Editable sources are in `src/`. No credit needed.

- humane.png: lapel pin, rounded square body with a sensor strip and a dotted projection beam below.
- friend.png: round pendant on a cord.
- rayban-display.png: thick-framed glasses with a small display in one lens, plus a wristband below.
- stream-ring.png: ring with a flat top carrying a three-hole microphone grille.
- android-xr.png: plain glasses with clear lenses, no display, one small camera dot.
- muse-charm.png: keychain fob on a split ring with a round tap button.
- openai.png: halftone disc with a question mark cut out. Deliberately no device: the product is unannounced.
